## Frequently asked questions

**Are the tracks compulsory?**
No. They are suggestions from the scientific areas to help you build a coherent study plan. You can follow a track only in part, combine courses from several areas or build your own plan, as long as it meets the curriculum rules.

**My interests don't fit any track.**
That's normal. Many students build a tailored profile — for example, with a strong component in electronics, computing, technology or medical physics. Talk to the guide of the area closest to your interests or to the coordination.

**A recommended course is not running this year.**
Courses that are not offered in the current academic year are flagged. Talk to the track guide to choose an alternative.

**Where are the official timetables and rules?**
In the [MEFT curriculum on Fénix](https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/cursos/meft21/curriculo). In case of any discrepancy, Fénix prevails.

**I found a mistake on this page.**
Please let the coordination know by email.
