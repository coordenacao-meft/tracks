  courses for students who want to follow a given area. Each track has a faculty member
  who is available to guide students' choices.

## How to use

1. **Choose an area** you are interested in and look at its core courses.
2. **Look at the specialisation options** and how they are spread across periods.
3. **Talk to the track guide** (or your future supervisor) before enrolling.
4. **Check on Fénix** which courses run this academic year and their timetables.

Your interests don't fit any track? That's normal — see [Contacts & FAQ]({{ '/en/contacts/' | relative_url }}).
